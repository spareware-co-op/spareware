# Spare Research: architecture map

*Stage: Idea. Named, not mapped yet.*

Replaces journal paywalls and the fees scientists pay to publish. Much research is publicly funded and peer-reviewed by scientists for free, yet a lot of it sits behind journal paywalls, and publishing it openly often costs the authors thousands in fees. Spare Research makes it free to read and free to publish, run at cost.

## Goals

- Free to read for anyone, anywhere.
- Free to publish; the only charge is running costs, shown on the open cost ledger.
- Peer review in the open.
- Papers that last: permanent links, and copies kept safe for the long term.

## Its parts (high level)

| Part | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Papers | Upload a paper with its data and code; every version is kept; free to read and download |
| Peer review | Reviewers invited for each paper; reviews published alongside it |
| Journals on top | A group of editors runs a journal as a list of papers they have reviewed and accepted, like the overlay journals that sit on arXiv |
| Search | Find and follow topics, authors and journals |
| Permanent links | A DOI for each paper and an ORCID iD for each author, so papers can be cited and found anywhere |
| Kept safe | Copies held by outside archives, so papers outlive any one server |

It uses the kit's accounts, messaging, member voting and open cost ledger.

## Learn from (read how they work, write our own)

- **arXiv:** free preprints for physics, maths, computing and more; new authors are endorsed by an existing author before their first paper.
- **Open Journal Systems** from the Public Knowledge Project: open-source journal software under the GPL, so we read how it works and write our own code; we never copy it.
- **Diamond open access** journals: free to read and free to publish, the model Spare Research follows.
- **Open data about papers:** OpenAlex (an open catalogue of research, CC0), Crossref (DOIs), ORCID (author IDs) and Jisc's Open Policy Finder, formerly Sherpa Romeo (what each publisher lets authors share).

## Built legally

Spare Research hosts only papers whose authors have the right to share them: their own work, papers under open licences, and the versions publishers allow authors to post. It never hosts copies of paywalled papers.
