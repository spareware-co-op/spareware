    Plan and costs

    Free for everyone
    Every app is freeNo subscription. Everything that runs on your computer costs nothing.
    AI tools on our servers cost what they cost usPaid after use, never more than the monthly limit you set. On-device AI is free.

    Your work stays yoursWe take no rights to it beyond syncing and sharing it when you ask. What you make with AI is yours too, as far as your country's law allows.
