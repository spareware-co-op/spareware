# Spare Construct status

**Stage: Mapping.** Roadmap phase 0 (Foundations) has not started.

- Architecture map written: [ARCHITECTURE.md](ARCHITECTURE.md), 23 sections at high and low level.
- 21 decisions open (table near the end of the map). No code yet.
- **Traffic and public transport is further along.** A working browser prototype, the [Transport demo](https://spareware.app/spare-construct/#plan:transport) (open it from the discipline button in the Spare Construct demo), already runs a strategic traffic model and a microsimulation on a live map. It was made to help a traffic engineer, who hasn't used it yet, so nobody from the field has checked it. The product is built from the core up on the shared engines, using the prototype to learn from.
- **Workshop, our mechanical and product design discipline, has a working prototype built by the geometry kernel.** Mapped as area [CON-A27](FEATURES.md#con-a27-workshop-parts-assemblies-and-drawings): parts drawn as sketches and built from features, a feature history to roll back and edit, parameters, assemblies whose joints move within limits, and drawings of both. The [Workshop demo](https://spareware.app/spare-construct/#plan:workshop) (07 on the discipline button) builds its parts with the kernel compiled for the browser ([construct-workshop](crates/construct-workshop)) and poses its assembly through the joints with the kernel's transforms, sizing its drawings from the kernel's boxes, and the app lists it beside Architecture and keeps its parts in the project's model, through the same edit log as walls and doors. Much of it waits for the kernel (booleans, sweeps, fillets, shells, the sketch solver, hidden lines, and hinges turned to angles other than quarter turns): the ribbon and the feature map name the kernel pieces each part waits for, and a feature or joint that waits or fails shows a rebuild report: the kernel's own structured error, with what would make it succeed.
- Design stage starts when an owner accepts decision notes for the first six decisions: kernel start, storage layout, entity store, incremental engine, renderer, multi-user.
