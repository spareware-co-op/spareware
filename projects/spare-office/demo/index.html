Native planting proposal: Maitai riverbank

For the Maitai Valley Residents' Association. Draft 3, 19 October 2026.

  1. Summary1
  2. Plants and costs1
  3. Planting zones2

1. Summary

We propose planting 695 native plants along 400 metres of the Maitai riverbank and 30 metres of the stream mouth. The plants will shade the water, hold the bank together and bring back birds. The work costs $4,193.48 including GST, which is about 7% under the budget the association set in August.

The planting day on Saturday 7 November is open to every whanau in the valley. Volunteers can sign up with the planting day form.1

2. Plants and costs

All plants are eco-sourced from the Nelson area. The table below is linked to the plant order sheet, so it updates when the sheet changes.

Linked from Sheets · Plant order 2026-27

3. Planting zones

Zone A sits below the footbridge and gets the tallest plants. Zone B is the steep bank, where harakeke and carex hold the soil. Zone C is the flat at the stream mouth, kept low so people can still see the water. Each zone have its own planting plan.

4. Timeline

  1. Site preparation and weed control, 27 to 30 October.
  2. Plants delivered by Tōtara Nursery, 5 November.
  3. Planting day with volunteers, Saturday 7 November, 9 am to 1 pm.
  4. Follow-up weeding, December 2026 to March 2027.

5. Next steps

Please confirm the proposal by Friday 30 October so we can place the plant order with the nursery.
