# Spare Social

Working name. One app for short videos, long videos, photos, posts, groups and messages, replacing TikTok, YouTube, Instagram and Facebook. Owned by its members, run at cost, no adverts, no secret algorithm. Stage: Mapping.

- [ARCHITECTURE.md](ARCHITECTURE.md): the map.
- [STATUS.md](STATUS.md): the work board.
- `notes/`: research and decision notes.

`crates/` and `app/` are added at the Design stage, following [the template](../_template/README.md). Video uses the shared `engine-media` in `projects/engines`.
