Who decides
  • Driver members vote on Spare Ride's fares, fees and rules.
  • Major changes, such as to the shared kit or the software licence, go to a vote of all members.
  • An urgent safety change can be made at once, then members confirm it within 30 days.
  • Before the first real fare vote, the co-op confirms competition law allows it.
