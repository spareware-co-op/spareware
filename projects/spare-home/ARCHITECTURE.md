# Spare Home: architecture map

*Stage: Mapping. Every choice below is open until an owner accepts a decision note.*

Selling a home yourself, with everything an agent's commission pays for available as separate, fairly priced services. Spare Home gives sellers the tools; it never acts as their agent. It runs on the shared marketplace loop; this map covers only what home sales add.

## Goals

- A seller can list, show, take offers and sell without paying a percentage commission.
- Buyers see the same listing quality they're used to, with more honest data.
- Every professional the seller needs (photographer, lawyer, valuer, building inspector, licensed agent if they want one) is booked through Spare Work at a published price.
- Floor plans can come straight from Spare Arch.

## What it uses from the shared kit

Accounts and ID checks, payments (for services, never the house price), maps, messaging, ratings, member voting, the open cost ledger, and the marketplace engine. Services are booked through Spare Work.

## Its own parts

### Listing

**High level:** photos, floor plans, description, open homes, documents (title, LIM, building reports), price or price indication, history.

**Low level:** listings searchable by map, price, size and features; alerts for buyers; floor plans imported from Spare Arch drawings.

**Discuss:** feeding listings to other property sites so sellers keep their reach.

### Viewings and offers

**High level:** buyers book open homes and private viewings; buyers register interest and send their lawyer's offer to the seller; the seller sees every offer in one place.

**Low level:** the offer register records who offered what and when; the binding sale agreement is drawn up by the parties' lawyers, never by the app.

**Discuss:** how multi-offer situations are run fairly.

### Seller help

**High level:** a step-by-step guide to selling privately, a checklist of legal steps, and the services marketplace.

## The line Spare Home doesn't cross

Anyone acting as an agent for someone else in a sale must be licensed. Spare Home provides tools and lets sellers book licensed professionals; it never negotiates or acts on a seller's behalf (piece HM01).
